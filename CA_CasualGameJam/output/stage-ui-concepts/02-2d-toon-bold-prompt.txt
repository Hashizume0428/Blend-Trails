Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: finished concept screenshot of the STAGE GAMEPLAY screen for COLOR TRACE, an existing mobile color-path puzzle; UI visual exploration, not a level-selection screen.
Output: one single high-resolution 9:16 portrait game screen, edge-to-edge screenshot; no phone, no device frame, no surrounding presentation sheet, no split panels. Crisp finished mobile UI, generous touch targets and strong hierarchy, restrained decoration.
Shared functional structure: top-right home icon; centered prominent exact title "STAGE 3"; main board at mid-screen; a compact red + blue = purple color-mixing legend beneath the board; bottom reset tray containing exactly three large buttons, left neutral circular-arrow reset labeled "ALL", middle blue circular-arrow reset, right red circular-arrow reset. The colored buttons reset their own color. Bottom controls within thumb reach and safe screen margins. Small supporting instruction "TRACE & MIX" near the title, and "RESET" above the bottom tray.
The board must remain flat, front-facing and geometrically precise: EXACTLY FIVE COLUMNS and FOUR ROWS, 20 equal square tiles, consistent gaps. Starting state, all tile interiors warm-white. Red circular starting dot centered in row 1 column 5, blue circular starting dot centered in row 4 column 1. Red target outline on row 1 column 3; blue target outline on row 4 column 3; purple target outlines on row 2 column 3 and row 3 column 3, EACH TILE INDIVIDUALLY OUTLINED with visible gap between them. Other tiles have quiet neutral borders. No route lines drawn yet. Board occupies about 86% of screen width and around 40% of the height, header about 20%, legend about 10%, controls around bottom 20%; use breathing space rather than extra widgets.
Reference intent: broad US-market casual mobile puzzle UI principles seen in Toon Blast, Candy Crush and Royal Match: instantly readable playfield, friendly rounded type, large color-coded controls, cheerful polished finish. Create an original interface using the existing game mechanics. No copied logos, no borrowed characters.
Text verbatim: "STAGE 3", "TRACE & MIX", "RESET", "ALL". No other text.
Avoid: currency, lives, timers, moves counters, ads, shop or event badges, hint buttons, stars, progress bars, extra gameplay mechanics, fantasy characters, candy pieces inside tiles, rotated board, isometric board, decorative illustration competing with the board, tiny illegible text, watermark.
Style/medium: unmistakably flat 2D cartoon UI with bold ink outlines and offset hard graphic shadows, energetic US casual puzzle look. Bright sky-blue background with a few simple very low-contrast 2D cloud-like abstract shapes outside the gameplay area, not a landscape. Sunny cream panels, dark indigo outlines and typography, crisp white iconography, playful large rounded lettering. No 3D rendering, no realistic lighting, no shiny materials, no bevels.
Board treatment: thick but neat indigo rounded frame with a flat cream inside, 20 white square tiles with fine navy borders and subtle flat offset shadows. Target tile outlines remain red blue and purple, clearly visible and distinct from the framing. Keep the board orderly and easy to count.
Header: strong playful cream-on-indigo stage pill, simple small sky-blue home button framed in indigo. Big readable rounded type.
Controls: three broad rounded square buttons with bold outlines and small solid offset shadows, ALL charcoal, blue bright cobalt, red coral. Pure white reset arrow pictograms. Mixing legend is a small cream outlined capsule.
Overall mood: buoyant illustrated arcade-toon, original and polished. Avoid comic captions or extra labels. The major difference from a quiet minimal screen is decisive line weight, energetic blue/cream color blocking and graphic depth, while remaining completely 2D.
